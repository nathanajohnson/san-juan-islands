San Juan Islands interactive — wildlife audio sources
=====================================================
Local educational use only (family demo). Short clips trimmed from
public field recordings. Prefer NPS / Archive.org / Freesound previews.

Species clip mapping (source → assets/sounds/<id>.mp3)

orca           NPS Natural Sounds — Killer Whale (Glacier Bay)
               https://www.nps.gov/subjects/sound/sounds-orca.htm
eagle          NPS Natural Sounds — Bald Eagle (Yellowstone)
               https://www.nps.gov/subjects/sound/sounds-bald-eagle.htm
seal           Internet Archive — Animals and Birds Sound Effects
               “Seal, Cape Fur Barks, Squeals”
               https://archive.org/details/animals-and-birds-sound-effects
porpoise       Internet Archive — same pack, “Dolphin, Bottlenose Clicks”
               (click-train stand-in for Dall’s porpoise echolocation)
deer           Freesound preview — deer vocalization
oystercatcher  Freesound preview — oystercatcher / shorebird
heron          Freesound preview — heron
fox            Internet Archive — Animals and Birds, “Fox”
rabbit         Freesound preview — rabbit
steller        Freesound preview — sea lion
murrelet       Freesound preview — seabird (murrelets are rarely recorded;
               closest available small-seabird coastal call)
salmon         Freesound preview — fish / water splash
bluebird       NPS Yellowstone — Mountain Bluebird
               (closest available bluebird field recording)
hermit         Freesound preview — crab / scuttle
sculpin        Freesound preview — underwater ambience
barnacle       Freesound preview — rocky shore waves
madrona        Freesound preview — forest birds / wind
kelp           Freesound preview — underwater
garry-oak      NPS Yellowstone — Dawn Chorus (meadow/oak habitat)
anemone        NPS Natural Sounds — Ocean (Olympic NP)
ochre-star     NPS Ocean
chiton         Freesound rocky waves
mussel         Freesound rocky waves
urchin         Freesound underwater
rockweed       NPS Ocean
sea-lettuce    Freesound rocky waves

- Curtin University orca WAV samples
- NPS Humpback, Stream, Steller’s Jay, Elk, Squirrel, Western Gull
